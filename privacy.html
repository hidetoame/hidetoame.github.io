Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 17, 2026

Hideto Ame Creator Tools is used to operate creator-owned social media workflows. The tools may process account profile information, media files, captions, draft metadata, upload status, and API tokens needed to complete authorized platform actions.

Data is used only to manage creator content workflows, authenticate with connected platforms, upload or publish content when requested, and check posting or account status.

Access tokens and account credentials are stored locally or in controlled private environments and are not sold or shared with third parties for advertising.

Data may be sent to platform APIs such as TikTok, YouTube, X, Meta, or note only when needed for the requested account operation.

Users may disconnect platform access by revoking the app authorization from the relevant platform account settings.

For privacy questions, contact the account owner through the public social profiles linked from the main creator profiles.
